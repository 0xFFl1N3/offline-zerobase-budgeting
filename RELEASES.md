# What's new

What changed for you in each release, newest first. The full technical list is in CHANGELOG.md.

## 0.15 – Bills that come back
2026-11-27

- Goals for bills that come back (insurance every March, car tax every year) can repeat: after the bill they start over by themselves.
- Goals with a month no longer ask for the full amount again after you paid the bill. Once the month has passed, the goal says whether it was met and offers to repeat it yearly, set a new one or remove it.

## 0.14 – Faster entry
2026-11-27

- The payee field suggests payees you have used before while you type, and fills in the category you used for them last time.

## 0.13 – More ways to assign
2026-11-27

- Auto-assign has a menu now: fund your goals, cover overspending, or assign the same as last month. Each shows how much it would assign before you pick it.

## 0.12 – Try it with sample data
2026-11-27

- New to the app? "Try with sample data" on the setup screen fills it with two years of made-up transactions, in English or German, ending today.
- A bar at the top reminds you it is sample data. "Set up my own budget" clears it and starts the setup.

## 0.11 – A clearer look
2026-11-27

- Overhauled look: the budget and the recurring transactions are tidier and easier to scan.
- Clearer wording: buttons and texts say plainly what they do, especially in German.
- Long transaction lists load faster: the newest 100 show first, and more are a click away.
- Charts move smoothly when you switch the time period or the grouping.

## 0.10 – Debts
2026-11-27

- Track loans and financing as debt accounts. Each debt gets its own category to budget the payments in, with the minimum payment as its goal.
- Choose which group a debt's category goes into.
- Pay a debt by transferring to it, or just book its category on a payment, typed in or from the bank import.
- The payoff plan shows what's left and when the last payment is due. Try a higher monthly amount or a one-off extra payment to see how much sooner you'd be done.
- Owe more on an existing debt, or correct it to what the lender says.
- "What changed" now shows a short summary per release.
- Tidier Budget: categories without a goal show just their name; "Set goal" appears when you point at the row.
- Fixed: reconciling a debt could change Ready to Assign.

## 0.9 – Safer saving
2026-11-27

- If a change can't be saved, for example because the device's storage is full, a message says so. Before, the change just didn't appear.
- Forms keep what you typed when saving fails, so you can try again.
- Several open tabs stay in sync: a change in one shows up in the others.

## 0.8 – Undo
2026-11-26

- Undo a delete: after deleting a transaction, account, category or goal, you have 10 seconds to bring it back.
- Click the version number to see what's new.

## 0.7 – Keeping your data safe
2026-11-26

- The app asks the browser to keep your data permanently. Settings shows whether it agreed.
- Automatic daily backup into a folder of your choice (Chrome and Edge on a computer).
- Age of money on the Analytics tab: how long your money sat in your accounts before you spent it.
- Click a pie slice or a top payee in Analytics to see its transactions.
- In the bank import, use a category for all other rows from the same payee with one click.

## 0.6 – German
2026-11-26

- The app is available in German. Choose the language on the setup screen or in Settings.
- Amounts and dates are written the way your language writes them, e.g. 1.234,56 €.
- Overspending works like YNAB: an overspent category starts the next month at zero, and the shortfall comes out of Ready to Assign.
- Closing an account or archiving a category pauses the recurring transactions that use it.

## 0.5 – Phones
2026-11-26

- A layout for phones: tabs at the bottom, the accounts list behind a menu button, and screens that fit.
- Install the app on your phone or computer from the browser. Once opened, it also works without internet.

## 0.4 – Reports and bank import
2026-11-26

- Dark mode.
- New reports: income vs. spending, net worth, spending trend and top payees, over 6, 12 or 24 months or all time.
- Reconcile accounts against your bank statement.
- Import transactions from your bank's CSV file. Categories are filled in from the last one used for each payee, and rows already imported are recognised.
- An offline version that opens straight from a single file.

## 0.3 – The first version
2026-11-26

- Budget every euro: accounts, category groups and categories, assigning money month by month, with unspent money rolling over.
- Goals: save up to an amount, optionally by a date, or set aside a monthly amount.
- Transactions with splits, transfers, filters and a running balance.
- Recurring transactions, with a reminder when one is due.
- Spending charts on the Analytics tab.
- Export and import of all your data as a backup file.
